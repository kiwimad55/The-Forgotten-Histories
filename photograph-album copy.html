The Photograph Album

DISCOVERY 1

In 2014, I knew nothing about my great-grandmother, Margaret Ann Lynch. All I knew was that she had come from County Roscommon, Ireland.

Then I received an unexpected message from someone I had never met.

She had found an old family photograph album at a church sale. Curious about the names written inside, she searched online and came across my family history research. Realising the album was connected to the family I was researching, she contacted me and kindly offered to send it to me. A few days later, the album arrived.

When the album arrived, I opened it to the first page. Beside a faded family photograph were handwritten notes identifying those pictured.

A name to follow
Margaret Ann Keon.

Until that moment, all I knew was that my great-grandmother, Jennie Lynch, had come from County Roscommon. Margaret Ann Keon gave me my first lead into Irish family history.

I still had no idea who Margaret Ann Keon was. The handwritten notes gave me names, places and relationships, but little else. They were simply clues. For the first time, though, I had a name to research.

The album page that first introduced me to the Keon family. The handwritten notes recreate the information as I first encountered it in 2014.

DISCOVERY 2

The Marriage Record

The next clue

I still knew nothing about Margaret Ann Keon herself, but the marriage record changed everything.

It confirmed that Margaret Ann Keon of Annfield had married David Lynch of Springfield, Elphin, on 31 May 1860 at Ardclare Church of Ireland, County Roscommon.

More importantly, it introduced fathers, witnesses, places and family names. Each one became another lead to follow.

What had begun with a single photograph had become a trail of records leading deeper into Ireland's past.

Key Discovery

This record revealed that Margaret Ann Keon's father was William Burne Keon.

He became the next person to investigate.

New Questions Raised

Who was William Burne Keon?

What was his story, and where had the Keon family come from?

Every name on the record opened another door.

Marriage record of David Lynch and Margaret Ann Keon, 31 May 1860.
