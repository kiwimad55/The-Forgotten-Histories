The Truth
of Sheemore

◆

A forgotten life. A fatal morning. A verdict that may not have told the whole story.

The Truth of Sheemore is inspired by the surviving record of an eighteenth-century Irish murder trial. Court testimony, newspaper reports, family history and the landscape of County Leitrim reveal the lives caught in a tragedy that unfolded on Sheemore Hill in October 1786.

The novel looks beyond the verdict, reconstructing the people behind the official record and asking how much of the truth was ever heard.

M. C. Widdowson

Every family leaves traces.

Most are eventually forgotten.

Through letters, court records, photographs and family history, these novels reconstruct the lives that history left behind.

For more than thirty years I have explored my own family history and helped others uncover theirs, following forgotten lives through archives, letters, court records and old photographs.

The Forgotten Histories series grew from those discoveries. Each novel begins with real people, real places and surviving historical records. Where the record falls silent, the story is carefully reconstructed from the evidence that remains.

How Forgotten Histories Began

History is rarely a series of isolated events. Every decision has a reason, every family has a story, and every generation inherits the consequences of those who came before. My research has been an attempt to understand not only what happened, but why.

The journey began unexpectedly when a family photograph album, rescued from a church jumble sale, arrived in my hands. On its first page was a name I had never heard before.
